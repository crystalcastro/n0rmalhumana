Selected work

Four campaigns for Heinsohn Xelerator, a B2B software and nearshoring company selling to US tech leaders on LinkedIn (2024–2025). My role: copy and concept, in close collaboration with the design team.

CASE 01

Heinsohn Xelerator · LinkedIn (company page) · 2024

Humanizing nearshoring

Problem

To US CTOs, nearshoring and staff augmentation feel like cold, distant transactions.

Insight

Their real fears are operational: time-zone gaps, communication and speed to scale.

Idea

Let design carry the emotion: a brand mascot, a deep-sea metaphor for murky outsourcing, and a two-line thesis: “Offshoring’s a chore. Why go far… when near is so clear?”

Check

Before launch, I tested the tone with two senior tech professionals. Both read it as clear, direct and fresh for the industry.

CASE 02

Heinsohn Xelerator · LinkedIn (company page) · 2024–2025

House vs. home: selling the value of UX

Problem

Many tech leaders believe design just makes software look pretty.

Insight

Ground it in money: hard-to-use software simply doesn’t sell.

Idea

The developer builds the house; UX/UI turns it into a home. “Digital ecosystems: you won’t just use them, you’ll live in them.”

AI in the process

I used AI to brainstorm ten analogies and chose “house vs. home” as the most universal and emotional.

Check

Two people outside design read the copy and explained UX back correctly: making an app feel like home.

CASE 03

Heinsohn Xelerator · LinkedIn (company page) · Oct–Nov 2024

Holidays with a strategy

Problem

Corporate holiday posts are flat, generic and invisible.

Insight

A calendar date can carry a real B2B fear without losing a professional tone.

Idea

Halloween: “Our developers won’t ghost you. *They were just busy eradicating some bugs.” Thanksgiving: nearshoring as “operating on the same working clock as yours.”

CASE 04

Heinsohn Xelerator · LinkedIn carousel · 2024

“You didn’t file your taxes. Your team of AI agents did.”

Problem

To busy B2B executives, “agentic AI” sounds like one more buzzword.

Insight

Nobody gets excited about “multiple specialized agents”. Everybody gets excited about never filing taxes again.

Idea

A carousel that leads with the benefit, then personifies four agents as teammates: coordinator, organizer, tax brain and checker. It closes with a question built for comments, not likes.
